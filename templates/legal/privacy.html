Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026 · Effective immediately

Summary Drop Sigma is a multi-tenant SaaS that helps e-commerce merchants run their dropshipping operations. We only collect what we need to run the service, never sell your data, and respect every applicable data-protection law including GDPR (EU) and CCPA (California).

1. Who we are

"Drop Sigma", "we", "us" and "our" refer to Drop Sigma, operating from Pakistan. We provide an order-management, vendor-coordination, and customer-support SaaS for Shopify and WooCommerce stores at dropsigma.com.

2. Data we collect

2.1 Information you give us directly

  • Account details — name, email, password (hashed), phone number (optional).
  • Team members — names, emails, roles you assign to your employees and vendors inside Drop Sigma.
  • Billing details — handled by our payment processor (Stripe / Razorpay); we never store raw card numbers.
  • Support correspondence — emails, chat messages and screenshots you send to our support team.

2.2 Information pulled from your connected store

When you connect a Shopify or WooCommerce store via OAuth, with your explicit permission we sync:

  • Orders (order number, line items, fulfilment status, payment status, totals, currency).
  • Customer details associated with those orders (name, email, phone, shipping address).
  • Products referenced in orders (title, variant, SKU).
  • Tracking and fulfilment events we push back when you take action inside Drop Sigma.
  • Inbox messages (Gmail / IMAP) when you separately authorize the email module.

We never read or store: customer passwords, raw payment instruments, or any product data that isn't tied to an order you manage.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • IP address, browser type, OS, and approximate location (for security + abuse prevention).
  • Usage events — which features you opened, when you logged in. We don't sell this to anyone.
  • Cookies — session cookie for login, CSRF cookie for security. No third-party advertising cookies.

3. How we use the data

We use the information above to:

  • Run the service you signed up for (sync orders, send AI replies, push tracking back to your store).
  • Authenticate you and your team members and prevent unauthorized access.
  • Send transactional emails (order alerts, password resets, billing receipts).
  • Improve product reliability (anonymized error reporting and performance monitoring).
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax invoices, court orders, GDPR / CCPA requests).

We do not use your or your customers' data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to delivering Drop Sigma.

5. Where we store data

Our primary database lives in Railway's US data center. Backups are encrypted at rest and kept for 30 days. If you're in the EU, your data may be transferred outside the EEA — we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as the legal basis.

7. Your rights

Regardless of where you live, you can email support@dropsigma.com to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold on you.
  • Rectify — ask us to fix anything inaccurate.
  • Delete — request erasure ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict / Object — pause processing while we investigate.
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format (JSON / CSV).

We respond within 30 days. Free of charge unless the request is "manifestly unfounded or excessive".

For Shopify merchants We also honour the official Shopify GDPR webhooks: customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact. When you uninstall Drop Sigma from your store, all related data is deleted within 48 hours automatically.

8. Security

  • TLS 1.2+ on every connection.
  • Passwords hashed with PBKDF2 (Django default).
  • OAuth tokens encrypted at rest.
  • Per-tenant scoping enforced on every API endpoint — no cross-tenant data leakage possible.
  • HMAC signature verification on every incoming webhook.
  • Annual third-party security audit.

9. Children

Drop Sigma is a B2B product. It is not directed at children under 16 and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll post any material changes on this page and notify you via email at least 14 days before they take effect.
