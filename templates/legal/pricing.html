Frequently asked questions

Why only one plan?

Because tier-shopping is a tax on your time. We picked the price and feature set that works for almost every dropshipping operation — 3 stores, unlimited orders, sourcing manager included — and skipped the per-feature upsell.

What if I need more than 3 stores?

Email support@dropsigma.com — we'll set up additional stores on a per-store add-on. The base plan covers the vast majority of merchants.

Are there per-order fees?

No. The $99/mo covers unlimited orders. We don't bill per order, per ticket, or per AI reply — flat fee, every month.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, and PayPal via Stripe.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes — pay annually and save 20% (works out to $79/mo, billed yearly). Email support@dropsigma.com to switch to annual.

Is there a setup fee?

Never. You can connect your first store and import orders in under 60 seconds, completely self-serve.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — see our Refund Policy. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on your first paid month, no questions asked.
