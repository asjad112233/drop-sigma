Frequently asked questions

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. If you don't upgrade, your account simply pauses (your data is kept for 90 days in case you come back).

Can I change plans later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Upgrades are prorated; downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.

What happens if I exceed my order limit?

We'll never block your store. Instead, we email you with a soft warning at 90% usage. Continued overages are billed at $0.05 per extra order, with the option to auto-upgrade if it makes sense.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple Pay, and PayPal via Stripe. Enterprise customers can also pay by wire transfer or annual invoice.

Do you offer annual discounts?

Yes — pay annually and save 20% on any plan. Contact sales for custom multi-year discounts on the Plus plan.

Is there a setup fee?

Never. You can connect your first store and import orders in under 60 seconds, completely self-serve.

Can I get a refund?

Yes — see our Refund Policy. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on your first paid month, no questions asked.
