Talk to sales
? Common questions

Answers, no sales pitch.

How is this different from Helpscout / Gorgias + a freight forwarder?
Those are 4 different tools. We're one. Plus we own the supply chain — when something goes wrong with a parcel, we own it end to end. With a freight forwarder, you're the one chasing 4 vendors at 2 AM.
Do you really charge $0 on sourcing? What's the catch?
No catch. We make money on the SaaS subscription. Our suppliers compete to win your business — better quotes = more orders, more orders = more SaaS subscribers. Aligned incentives. No surprise markups.
What if my supplier ghosts me mid-production?
Every supplier in our network is verified, KYC'd, and posts a security deposit before joining. If they ghost, we re-source from the network — same SKU, same lead time, no penalty to you. Has happened twice in 18 months.
Will the AI sound like me, or like a generic chatbot?
It sounds like you because you trained it. 50 questions: refund policy, shipping rules, tone (friendly vs formal), signoff style, what to escalate. Tenants who edit their AI drafts ≤10% of the time after week 2 are the norm, not the exception.
Can I cancel anytime? Lock-in?
Cancel anytime, no questions asked. Your data exports as JSON the moment you ask. No annual contracts, no minimum commits, month-to-month billing. You're in control.
Does the branded tracking work on my domain?
On Starter: tracks via track.dropsigma.com/{your-tracking-id}. On Pro and Scale: white-label CNAME — track.yourbrand.com. Setup takes one DNS record.
