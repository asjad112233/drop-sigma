## Section A — App Name

**Shopify rule:** Cannot contain "Shopify" or any Shopify trademark. Should be unique, brandable, and pronounceable.

### Recommendation: keep **Drop Sigma**

It's already a registered domain (dropsigma.com), short, brandable, and trademark-safe. The two-word structure (`Drop` + `Sigma`) reads cleanly and gives room for sub-product naming later.

### Submission name (use this exact string in Partner Dashboard)

> **Drop Sigma — AI Email & Returns**

This is the legal app name plus a descriptive suffix. Shopify allows up to 30 characters in the listing title; this lands at 29.

### Backup options (if Shopify reviewer flags the name)

1. **Drop Sigma: AI Replies & RMA** (29 chars)
2. **Drop Sigma Customer Suite** (25 chars)

Pick option 1 if the reviewer asks you to make the value proposition clearer; pick option 2 if they want a more "platform-y" tone.

Paste the section below into the "Detailed description" field. Shopify renders markdown.

```markdown
## Stop drowning in customer-service email.

Drop Sigma is the AI-powered customer operations layer for Shopify stores. It reads every inbound email, classifies it (refund, shipping, "needs human"), drafts a reply in your brand voice using Anthropic Claude, and lets you send with one click. Pair that with a complete returns workflow, vendor coordination, and a built-in team chat — and you have everything you need to run support without hiring.

### Key features

- **AI-drafted email replies** — Every customer email gets a ready-to-send draft generated by Claude, tuned on your past responses. You approve, edit, or send as-is.
- **Smart classification** — Inbound mail is auto-sorted into refund, shipping, product question, or "needs human" — your inbox is triaged before you open it.
- **Full returns / RMA workflow** — Customer-facing return form, internal approval flow, automated status emails, and one-click Stripe refunds.
- **Vendor management** — Assign orders to suppliers, track which vendor owes which tracking number, and give each vendor their own portal login.
- **27 email templates x 9 order-status categories** — Pre-built, fully editable templates for every touchpoint (order confirmation, shipped, delivered, refund-issued, etc.).
- **Multi-store** — Connect every Shopify, WooCommerce, and Gmail account you own into one unified dashboard.
- **Team chat built in** — Real-time chat for your support team without sending them to Slack.
- **Self-learning AI** — The more you correct drafts, the closer they get to your voice. No prompt engineering required.

### How it works

1. **Connect** — Install from the Shopify App Store, click "Connect Shopify" in your Drop Sigma dashboard, and authorize. Order sync starts immediately.
2. **Configure** — Connect your Gmail (one click), pick your email templates, set your return policy.
3. **Watch it run** — Open Drop Sigma each morning, review the drafts the AI prepared overnight, and send. Most merchants clear their full inbox in under 15 minutes.

### Why merchants love it

- **Save 10+ hours per week** per support agent on email triage and drafting.
- **Refund response time drops from days to minutes** with a single approve-and-send flow.
- **No more lost vendor tracking** — every shipment is mapped to a supplier with auto-reminders.
- **One dashboard, every store** — multi-tenant support means agencies and multi-brand operators run all clients from one login.
- **Built for solo founders and small teams** — no enterprise contract, no sales call.

### Integrations

- **Shopify** — Order + customer + product + fulfillment sync (read/write).
- **Gmail** — Real-time inbox sync via Google Pub/Sub; replies go out from your own Gmail address.
- **WooCommerce** — Connect WooCommerce stores alongside Shopify in the same dashboard.
- **Stripe** — One-click refunds for direct-checkout returns (optional).
- **Anthropic Claude** — Powers all AI drafting and classification.

### Pricing

**Drop Sigma is free to install from the Shopify App Store.** Optional paid plans (more AI credits, more team seats, priority support) are available at dropsigma.com via Stripe. You can use the free plan forever — no credit card, no trial expiration.

### Support

- Email: support@dropsigma.com
- Response time: under 12 hours, Mon–Sat
- Live chat from inside the app, Mon–Fri
- Help center: dropsigma.com/support
```

## Section F — Category Selection

Shopify currently surfaces these categories under "Customer support" and "Store management." Use both slots.

- **Primary category:** `Customer support` → sub-category `Live chat & email`
- **Secondary category:** `Store management` → sub-category `Returns and exchanges`

**Rationale:** AI email drafting is the headline feature, so primary belongs in Customer Support. RMA is the second-strongest feature and pulls in a different search audience, so use the secondary slot for Returns. Do not pick "Email marketing" — that audience is looking for Klaviyo-style campaign tools and will give you bad reviews.

## Section I — Step-by-Step Partner Dashboard Submission Guide

> URL: https://partners.shopify.com → Apps → your app

### Step 1. Create the app (if not already)

1. Partner Dashboard → **Apps** → **Create app** → **Create app manually**.
2. **App name:** `Drop Sigma — AI Email & Returns`
3. App type: **Public app** (so it's listable in the App Store).
4. Click **Create app**.

### Step 2. App URL + redirect URLs

In the new app, go to **Configuration**:

- **App URL:** `https://dropsigma.com/`
- **Allowed redirection URL(s)** — add this single entry:
  - `https://dropsigma.com/stores/api/shopify-callback/`

Click **Save**.

### Step 3. API access & scopes

Go to **App Setup → API access**. Under **Access scopes**, select these exact scopes:

- `read_orders`
- `write_orders`
- `read_customers`
- `read_products`
- `read_fulfillments`
- `write_fulfillments`

For each scope, paste the matching justification from your `SHOPIFY_SCOPE_JUSTIFICATION.md` document into the "Justification" field. Shopify reads these — vague justifications get rejected. Use one sentence per scope explaining the user-facing feature that requires it (e.g., `write_fulfillments` → "Required to push tracking numbers from connected vendor portals back to the merchant's Shopify orders so customers receive shipped notifications.").

### Step 4. Webhooks (mandatory)

In **App Setup → Webhooks → Compliance webhooks**, set:

| Webhook | URL |
|---|---|
| Customer data request | `https://dropsigma.com/orders/webhook/shopify/customers-data-request/` |
| Customer redact | `https://dropsigma.com/orders/webhook/shopify/customers-redact/` |
| Shop redact | `https://dropsigma.com/orders/webhook/shopify/shop-redact/` |

In **App Setup → Webhook subscriptions**, add:

- Topic: `app/uninstalled` → URL: `https://dropsigma.com/orders/webhook/shopify/app-uninstalled/`

**Before submission, test each webhook** — Shopify's reviewer will hit them and reject the app if any return non-200.

### Step 6. Pricing

Go to **Distribution → Pricing**.

- **Pricing model:** `Free`
- **External pricing disclosure** (this field appears once you select Free + tick "I offer paid plans outside Shopify"): paste verbatim:

> Drop Sigma offers optional paid plans billed at dropsigma.com via Stripe. The Shopify App Store install is always free, and you can use Drop Sigma indefinitely on the free plan with no credit card required. Paid plans unlock additional AI credits, team seats, and priority support.

### Step 7. App distribution type

Under **Distribution → App distribution**, set:

- **Embedded in Shopify admin?** → **No**
- **Distribution type** → **Standalone app with external billing**

This is the critical setting that lets you list a non-embedded app. If you skip this, the reviewer will ask you to embed.

### Step 8. Submit for review

1. Top right → **Submit for review**.
2. Shopify will run an automated check first (broken URLs, missing webhooks, missing assets). Fix anything flagged.
3. Once it passes the auto-check, a human reviewer is assigned. Typical wait: **5–10 business days** for first response, **1–3 weeks** total to approval.
4. The reviewer will message you in the Partner Dashboard. **Respond within 24 hours** — slow responses extend the queue. Most apps go through 1–3 revision rounds.
5. Common review feedback to anticipate:
   - "Demonstrate the value of each scope on a fresh dev store" → record a short Loom on a fresh dev install showing the feature each scope unlocks.
   - "Detailed description must not promise features not in the app" → make sure every bullet in Section D is live.
   - "External billing must be clearly disclosed before install" → ensure your dropsigma.com onboarding shows free-vs-paid before any charge.
